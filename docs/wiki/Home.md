# PackagedAuto Terminals

AE2 terminals that browse and edit **PackagedAuto** recipes from anywhere on the ME network.

Without this mod, encoding a recipe means walking: fill a **Package Recipe Encoder**, take the
**Package Recipe Holder** out, go to the Packager, go to the Unpackager, and walk again for every
change. This mod removes that walk.

## Requirements

**AE2 Unofficial Extended Life** and **PackagedAuto** are required. Everything else is optional
and detected by modid: JEI, PackagedExCrafting, PackagedAvaritia, PackagingProvider, AE2WUT, and
the pair **AE2 Fluid Crafting** plus **PackagedFluidCrafting** for fluids.

Without an addon, the feature disappears and nothing crashes.
